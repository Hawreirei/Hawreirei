## Inline & display math

The quadratic formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ solves $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$.
Subscripts and stars stay intact: $x_1 \cdot x_2 = \frac{c}{a}$ and $a * b_{n}$.

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{3}
$$

$$

## A worked problem

What is the last digit of $7^{2026}$?

<Solution lang={props.lang}>
The last digits of $7^n$ repeat every 4: $7, 9, 3, 1, 7, 9, \dots$

Since $2026 = 4 \cdot 506 + 2$, the last digit of $7^{2026}$ is the same as for $7^2 = 49$.

$$
7^{2026} \equiv 7^{2} \equiv 9 \pmod{10}
$$

**Answer: 9.**
</Solution>
