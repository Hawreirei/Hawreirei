Look at the sequence:

$$
2,\ 6,\ 12,\ 20,\ 30,\ \dots
$$

**What is the next term?**

<Hint>
Look at the differences between terms. Or try writing each term as a product of two consecutive numbers.
</Hint>

<Solution>
Differences: $4,\ 6,\ 8,\ 10$ — they grow by 2, so the next difference is $12$.

Next term: $30 + 12 = 42$.

Another way: each term is $n(n+1)$:
$1\cdot2,\ 2\cdot3,\ 3\cdot4,\ 4\cdot5,\ 5\cdot6$, so the 6th term is $6 \cdot 7 = 42$.

**Answer: $42$.**
</Solution>
