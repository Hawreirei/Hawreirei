Solve the simultaneous equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + y &= 11 \\
x - y &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

<Hint>
Add the two equations — the $y$ terms cancel.
</Hint>

<Solution>
Add the equations:

$$
(2x + y) + (x - y) = 11 + 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 3x = 12 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 4
$$

Substitute into $x - y = 1$: $4 - y = 1$, so $y = 3$.

Check: $2(4) + 3 = 11$ ✓ and $4 - 3 = 1$ ✓

**Answer: $x = 4,\ y = 3$.**
</Solution>
