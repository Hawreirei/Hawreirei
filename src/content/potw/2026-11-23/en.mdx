**What is the last digit of $7^{2026}$?**

<Hint>
Work out the last digits of $7^1, 7^2, 7^3, 7^4, 7^5, \dots$ and look for a pattern.
</Hint>

<Solution>
The last digits of $7^n$ repeat every 4: $7,\ 9,\ 3,\ 1,\ 7,\ 9,\ \dots$

Since $2026 = 4 \cdot 506 + 2$, the last digit is the same as for $7^2 = 49$.

$$
7^{2026} \equiv 7^{2} \equiv 9 \pmod{10}
$$

**Answer: $9$.**
</Solution>
