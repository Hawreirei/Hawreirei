A cake is cut into **8 equal pieces**. Ani eats $\frac{3}{8}$ of the cake and Budi eats $\frac{1}{4}$ of it.

**What fraction of the cake is left?**

<Hint>
Write $\frac{1}{4}$ in eighths first, so both fractions have the same denominator.
</Hint>

<Solution>
Use a common denominator: $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{8}$.

Eaten: $\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$.

Left: $1 - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{8}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8}$.

**Answer: $\frac{3}{8}$ of the cake** (3 of the 8 pieces).
</Solution>
